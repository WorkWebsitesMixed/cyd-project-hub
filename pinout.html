The "free" pins are limited. The CYD only breaks out a handful of usable GPIO on its JST connectors — commonly 22, 27, 35 (input-only) plus power. If a project needs several free pins (I²C sensor + more), plan around these. Pins 34/35/36/39 are input-only and have no internal pull-ups.
Strapping pins (0, 2, 12, 15) affect how the ESP32 boots — most are used by the display, so avoid repurposing them and don't hold them at odd levels during reset. To dim the screen, drive GPIO21 with PWM.

Board revisions vary

There are several CYD variants (resistive vs capacitive touch, single/dual USB, USB-C vs micro-USB, and occasionally a different display controller). The pins above match the common ESP32-2432S028R. Always confirm against the authoritative PINS.md / SETUP.md in the official repo ↗ before wiring anything.
